Travii: Your Personal Travel Book

Turn your flights and trips into beautiful, keepsake-style boarding passes. Travii lets you log every flight and land trip, creating a private digital collection of travels right on your phone.

What is Travii?

Travii is a simple travel tracking app that transforms your journeys into beautiful, boarding pass-style tickets. Whether you're a frequent flyer or occasional traveler, keep your travels organized and accessible—no clutter, no complexity. Meet Ravi, your travel companion, who helps you make the most of your journeys.

Key Features

• Flight & Trip Logging — Quickly add flights and land trips with departure, arrival, and optional details like date and airline.

• Boarding Pass-Style Memories — Each flight and trip becomes a beautiful, keepsake-style ticket you can view and cherish.

• Trip Organization — Group related flights and trips together. Perfect for multi-leg journeys or organizing by vacation.

• Travel Statistics — Track trips taken and see your travel patterns at a glance.

• 100% Private & Local — All data stays on your phone. No cloud, no tracking, no ads. Your memories are yours alone.

Why Travii?

We believe travel moments deserve to be kept safe and simple. Travii gives you a clean, distraction-free way to document your adventures without complex features or data collection concerns.

Perfect For

• Anyone who wants to preserve travel events in one place
• Frequent travelers looking for a simple trip log
• People planning future travel based on past adventures
• Travelers who value privacy and offline-first design

How It Works

-Tap "Add Flight" or "Add Trip"
-Enter your departure and arrival locations
-Add optional details (date, airline, notes)
-Your entry is saved as a beautiful boarding pass-style ticket
-Add comments with Ravi to capture stories and moments
-Browse your collection anytime, organize into trips
-Built for Privacy

No account needed. No location tracking. No data sharing. Your travel history stays entirely on your device.
